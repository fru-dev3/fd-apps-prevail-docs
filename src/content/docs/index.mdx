**Every part of your life — wealth, health, tax, career — is a folder of markdown.** Prevail is a native app that opens one, grounds an AI in that domain's real history, and lets you fan a hard question out to a **council** of models at once. A chair reads every reply and writes one decisive answer, with the _disagreement_ called out — because that's usually where the real decision is.

It runs on your machine. Nothing leaves unless you turn on an integration. And it **learns you**: every chat is remembered as markdown you own, distilled into per-domain memory that's fed back into the next conversation — so it gets sharper the more you use it.

## Why Prevail

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="It learns you" icon="open-book">
    Every chat is captured as an *intent* and distilled into per-domain memory. The app gets sharper the more you use it — your AI, shaped to your life.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A council, not one voice" icon="comment-alt">
    Fan one question to several models in parallel. A chair synthesizes a verdict, and disagreement gets its own panel — that's where the value is.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Local-first by default" icon="approve-check">
    Your vault, chats, and memory stay on your machine. Nothing leaves unless you turn on an integration. Run sensitive domains entirely on a local model.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Domain = folder" icon="document">
    `wealth/`, `health/`, `tax/`. Plain markdown you can edit anywhere and sync with git, iCloud, or Tailscale. No database, no lock-in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Any model, one app" icon="puzzle">
    Installed CLIs (Claude · Codex · Antigravity · Ollama) **or** bring-your-own via OpenRouter — one key, 200+ models. Switch per turn; context carries.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Free & open source" icon="heart">
    GPL-3.0, developed in the open. Signed and notarized builds for macOS and Windows. Self-build and audit every line.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

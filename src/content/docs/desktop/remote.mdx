Prevail lives on your machine, but you don't have to be at your machine to use it. There are two ways off the desk — both **off by default** and locked down when you turn them on.

## The WebUI

The desktop app can serve the **exact same UI** to a web browser — no separate mobile app, no duplicate interface. The frontend talks to the backend through one bridge (Tauri IPC on the desktop, HTTP/SSE in the browser), so the browser view *is* the app.

<Steps>

1. **Settings → Remote (WebUI)** — turn it on.

2. Prevail binds to **loopback only** and enforces an **allowlist** — it isn't exposed to your network.

3. **Reach it over [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com).** Your phone joins the same tailnet as your machine and opens the WebUI as if it were local — encrypted, private, no ports opened to the public internet.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Loopback + allowlist by design">
The WebUI never listens on a public interface. It's loopback-bound and allowlisted so the only way in is a private network you control (like Tailscale). See [Security & threat model](/reference/security/).
</Aside>

## The Telegram bridge

For quick questions from your phone without opening a browser, the **gateway** bridges Prevail to Telegram. Ask a question in a chat, get the council's verdict back — with a **mandatory chat-ID allowlist** so only you can talk to it.

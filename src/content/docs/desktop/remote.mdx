Prevail lives on your machine, but you don't have to be at your machine to use it. There are two ways off the desk — both **off by default** and locked down when you turn them on.

## The WebUI

The desktop app can serve the **exact same UI** to a web browser — no separate mobile app, no duplicate interface. The frontend talks to the backend through one bridge (Tauri IPC on the desktop, HTTP/SSE in the browser), so the browser view *is* the app.

<Steps>

1. **Settings → Remote (WebUI)** — turn it on, with **Reachable from other devices** on (it is by default).

2. With [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com) installed, Prevail binds to this Mac's **Tailscale address only**: reachable from every device on your tailnet, encrypted end to end, never on the public internet. Without Tailscale it uses your local Wi-Fi network instead.

3. The settings page shows the address and a **QR code**. Scan it with your phone, sign in, and you are in the real app.

</Steps>

## Install it on your phone

The WebUI is a full installable web app, so it runs as its own full-screen app with an icon, no App Store needed, and it stays signed in.

- **iPhone (Safari):** open the address, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android (Chrome):** open the address, tap the menu, then **Install app**.

On a phone the sidebar becomes a drawer behind the **☰** button; everything else is the same app.

<Aside type="caution" title="Private network only, by design">
The WebUI never listens on a public interface. It binds to loopback, or to your Tailscale address, and rejects requests from any other host name, so the only way in is a private network you control. Prefer Tailscale over plain Wi-Fi: it encrypts the connection. See [Security & threat model](/reference/security/).
</Aside>

## The Telegram bridge

For quick questions from your phone without opening a browser, the **gateway** bridges Prevail to Telegram. Ask a question in a chat, get the council's verdict back — with a **mandatory chat-ID allowlist** so only you can talk to it.

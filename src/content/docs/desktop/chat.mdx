The Chat and Council tabs are where most of the work happens. Everything here is **grounded in a domain** — Prevail reads that domain's state, history, and memory before it answers, so you don't repeat yourself.

## Pick a domain

Click any domain in the left sidebar — `wealth`, `health`, `tax`, `career`. The chat is now scoped to it. Pin the ones you use most so they sit at the top; add a new one with **`+ new domain`**.

Each domain keeps its own conversation, its own memory, and its own default model and framework. Switching domains switches context completely.

→ [Domains](/vault/domains/)

## Chat with one model

<Steps>

1. **Pick a model** in the input bar — an installed CLI (Claude, Codex, Antigravity, Ollama) or any model via OpenRouter.

2. **Type and send.** The reply streams in live, grounded in the domain.

3. **Switch models mid-conversation** if you want a second take — context carries across the switch.

</Steps>

Use the **framework** and **lens** pickers in the input bar to shape *how* the answer comes back — Bottom Line Up Front, a decision matrix, a devil's-advocate pass, and so on.

→ [Frameworks](/council/frameworks/) · [Lenses](/council/lenses/)

## Convene the council

Switch to the **Council** tab — or choose *Council* in the model selector — and ask. Prevail fans your question out to every available model **in parallel**:

- Each model answers independently.
- A **chair** model reads all the replies and writes one synthesized verdict.
- A **divergence panel** surfaces exactly where the panelists disagreed.

<Aside type="tip">
The verdict is the answer; the divergence is the insight. When two strong models split, the reason they split is usually the real decision in front of you.
</Aside>

You choose how many models sit on the council and which one chairs it in [Settings → Council](/desktop/settings/).

→ [How the council works](/council/overview/) · [Chair, verdicts & divergence](/council/verdicts/)

## It remembers, automatically

You never have to "save" a chat. The moment you send a message it becomes a durable **intent**, and Prevail distills each domain's conversations into memory that's read back into the next one. Come back tomorrow and it already knows where you left off.

→ [Memory & self-learning](/desktop/memory/)

## History & skills

The input bar exposes a couple of shortcuts:

- **`/ history`** — jump back through this domain's past turns.
- **`/ skills`** — run a saved [skill](/vault/skills/) for this domain, or distill the current conversation into a new one.

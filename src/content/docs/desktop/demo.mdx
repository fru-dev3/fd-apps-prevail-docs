Prevail is **demo-first**. There's nothing to configure before you can try the whole app — every launch starts in a realistic, pre-populated vault.

## Demo mode

On first launch (and any time you switch back to it), Prevail opens a synthetic **household vault** — a complete family with ~11 domains, realistic data, and chat history already in place. You can:

- Browse domains and read their state and memory.
- Chat and convene the council against real-looking context.
- Open the benchmark viewer with runs already scored.

Nothing in the demo is real personal data, so explore freely. A banner in the status bar shows you're in demo mode, with a one-click **"set up your own vault."**

<Aside type="note">
Demo mode is non-destructive. Switching to your own vault never touches the demo, and you can flip back any time under Settings → Demo Mode.
</Aside>

## Starter packs

When you're ready for your own vault, you don't start from an empty folder. Import a **starter pack** — a ready-made set of domains tuned to a situation:

| Pack | Good for |
|---|---|
| **Family** | A household: wealth, health, kids, home, calendar |
| **General** | A balanced default set of life-domains |
| **High-Income** | Tax, wealth, equity, and planning-heavy domains |
| **Freelancer** | Clients, invoicing, taxes, pipeline |
| **Creator** | Content, brand, audience, monetization |
| **Small Business Owner** | Operations, finances, team, growth |
| **Student** | Courses, applications, finances, career |

Each pack drops in pre-structured domains you can rename, prune, or extend — every one is just a folder of markdown.

## Setting up your own vault

<Steps>

1. **Settings → Vault** (or click the demo banner). Choose a folder for your vault, or let Prevail create one.

2. **Pick a starter pack** for your situation — or start minimal and add domains as you go.

3. **Start chatting.** From here, every conversation is captured into *your* vault and distilled into memory.

</Steps>

<Aside type="tip" title="Importing a pack while in demo mode">
If you import a pack while still in demo mode, Prevail walks you through vault setup first — so the pack lands in your real vault and nothing gets lost.
</Aside>
